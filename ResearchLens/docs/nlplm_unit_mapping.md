# NLPLM Syllabus Unit-Wise Mapping — ResearchLens

This document maps each unit of the **Natural Language Processing and Language Models (NLPLM)** syllabus to its working implementation and demonstration workflow in **ResearchLens**.

| Unit | Syllabus Topic | ResearchLens Feature | Implementation File | How to Demonstrate During Evaluation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Unit I** | Tokenization & Text Normalization | Paper Analyzer (Sections A & B) | `backend/app/services/preprocessing.py`, `frontend/src/pages/AnalyzerPage.tsx` | Open **Paper Analyzer**, load a paper or paste an abstract, click **Analyze Text**, and inspect token list, count, and normalized output. |
| **Unit I** | Stop-word Removal | Paper Analyzer (Section C) | `backend/app/services/preprocessing.py` | View separated lists of removed stop words and remaining content tokens. |
| **Unit I** | Stemming & Lemmatization | Paper Analyzer (Sections D & E) | `backend/app/services/preprocessing.py` | Compare `PorterStemmer` vs `WordNetLemmatizer` (with rule fallback) side-by-side tables. |
| **Unit I** | Bag of Words & N-gram Analysis | Paper Analyzer (Sections F & G) | `backend/app/services/preprocessing.py` | Inspect `CountVectorizer` vocabulary frequencies and top unigrams, bigrams, and trigrams. |
| **Unit I** | Regular Expressions | Paper Analyzer (Section H) | `backend/app/services/preprocessing.py` | Click **Analyze Text** on the default text containing an email, year (`2025`), and percentage (`14.5%`) to see regex matches. |
| **Unit I** | Morphology & Finite Automata / FST | Paper Analyzer (Sections I & J) | `backend/app/services/preprocessing.py` | Inspect inflectional vs derivational affix table, `-ing` FSA state acceptance, and FST base-form transducer examples. |
| **Unit II** | Part-of-Speech (POS) Tagging | NLP Lab (Section A) | `backend/app/services/pos_tagger.py`, `frontend/src/pages/NlpLabPage.tsx` | Open **NLP Lab**, click **Run POS, TF-IDF, N-gram & IE**, and inspect the Word \| POS Tag \| Explanation table. |
| **Unit II** | TF-IDF & Document Retrieval | NLP Lab (Section B) & Smart Search | `backend/app/services/tfidf.py`, `frontend/src/pages/SmartSearchPage.tsx` | View top-10 TF-IDF weighted terms in NLP Lab, or run a query in **Smart Search** to see cosine-ranked papers. |
| **Unit II** | N-gram Language Model | NLP Lab (Section C) | `backend/app/services/ngram.py` | Enter a 1–2 word context (e.g., `deep learning`) and view Laplace-smoothed next-word probabilities from the corpus. |
| **Unit II** | Information Extraction (NER/Rules) | NLP Lab (Section D) | `backend/app/services/keyword_extraction.py` | Inspect extracted research methods, algorithms, technologies, and domains. |
| **Unit II** | Lexical Semantics, WSD (Lesk) & Word Similarity | NLP Lab (Sections E, F, G) | `backend/app/services/wordnet_service.py` | Enter target word `bank` with context sentence and compare word `institution` to view WordNet relations, Lesk sense, and Wu-Palmer score. |
| **Unit III** | Semantic Similarity & Concept Analysis *(Modular)* | Semantic Analysis Page | `backend/app/services/semantic_service.py`, `frontend/src/pages/SemanticPage.tsx` | Open **Semantic Analysis**, compare two sentences for similarity %, and analyze an abstract for concepts and contextual word meaning. |
| **Unit IV** | Discourse Cohesion & Structure | Discourse & Pragmatics (Part 1) | `backend/app/services/discourse_service.py`, `frontend/src/pages/DiscoursePage.tsx` | Open **Discourse & Pragmatics**, click **Analyze Discourse** to view cohesive devices and heuristic sentence roles. |
| **Unit IV** | Deixis, Endophora/Exophora, Speech Acts, Pragmatic Markers & Face | Discourse & Pragmatics (Part 1) | `backend/app/services/discourse_service.py` | Inspect detected deictic words, anaphora/cataphora/exophora examples, speech act classification, and face theory examples. |
| **Unit IV** | Adjacency Pairs, Implicature & Cooperative Principle | Discourse & Pragmatics (Part 2) | `backend/app/services/discourse_service.py` | Click **Analyze Conversation** on the sample dialogue to view Question→Answer pairs, implied meaning, and Grice's 4 maxims. |
| **Unit V** | Hugging Face Summarization Pipeline & Fallback | AI Summarizer | `backend/app/services/summarization_service.py`, `frontend/src/pages/SummarizerPage.tsx` | Open **AI Summarizer**, select length and prompt template, and generate a summary (uses HF pipeline or TF-IDF extractive fallback). |
| **Unit V** | Summarization Evaluation (ROUGE-1/2/L) | AI Summarizer (Evaluation) | `backend/app/services/evaluation_service.py` | Click **Compute ROUGE Scores** after generating a summary to inspect Precision, Recall, and F1 for ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2, and ROUGE-L. |
| **Unit V** | Prompt Engineering & LLM Concepts | AI Summarizer (Templates & Guide) | `frontend/src/pages/SummarizerPage.tsx` | Switch between the 5 prompt templates (beginner, researcher, bullets, methodology, findings) and review the LLM concepts cards. |
